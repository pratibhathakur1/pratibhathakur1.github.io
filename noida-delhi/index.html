University life.

Noida brought university campuses, laboratories and a more research-oriented academic environment.

Noida & Delhi

Research internship · JNU

A Delhi chapter.

During my research internship at Jawaharlal Nehru University, I worked on the low-temperature magnetic properties of pyrochlore iridates.

It was an early chapter in my experience of experimental research.

The laboratory and classroom

Research and teaching.

My research focused on luminescent silicate and oxide nanophosphors, their structure, defects and optical behaviour.

Alongside research, I began teaching undergraduate students. This was where the laboratory and the classroom gradually became equally important parts of my work.
