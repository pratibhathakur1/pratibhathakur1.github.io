Chandigarh

DAV College, Sector 10 · Chandigarh

A City Beyond the Mountains.

After completing school in Kullu, I moved to Chandigarh to pursue my graduation.

I studied at DAV College, Sector 10, Chandigarh, affiliated with Panjab University. It was my first sustained experience of living and studying away from the mountains.
Chandigarh

Living in Chandigarh

City life.

Chandigarh introduced me to a different rhythm of life—planned roads, tree-lined avenues, college corridors and people from different cultures and ethnicities.

It was my first experience of living in a city shaped by movement, diversity and everyday encounters.

Chandigarh · Rose Garden

Rose Garden.

The Rose Garden brought a quieter side of the city into my college years—open paths, old trees and colour within Chandigarh’s carefully planned landscape.
