Childhood · Kullu Valley

Beside the Beas.

I grew up in a small village in Kullu Valley, beside the Beas River. Our pahadi home was surrounded by tall trees, with the Manali–Delhi Highway running next to it.

The Beas flowed just beyond the road, and the mountains stood behind it. This is where my story began.

The Beas.

The river was part of the landscape of everyday life. Beyond the road, the Beas moved through the valley, with the mountains rising quietly behind it.
